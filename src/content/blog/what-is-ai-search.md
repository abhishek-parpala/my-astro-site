# What Is AI Search?

AI search is changing the way people find information online.

Instead of simply returning a list of links, AI-powered search systems can understand a question, retrieve information, and generate a direct answer.

## How is AI search different?

Traditional search usually presents a ranked list of pages.

AI search can combine information from multiple sources and produce a synthesized response.

Examples include:

- ChatGPT
- Google AI Overviews
- Gemini
- Perplexity

## Why does this matter for businesses?

A potential customer may no longer discover a company by clicking the first Google result.

They might ask an AI system:

> "What are the best SEO agencies for B2B SaaS?"

If a brand is not present in the sources and entities that AI systems use, it may never enter that conversation.

## What can businesses do?

Businesses can improve their AI search visibility by:

- Publishing useful content
- Building topical authority
- Earning relevant mentions
- Strengthening their brand entity
- Creating content that clearly answers buyer questions

AI search does not eliminate SEO.

It expands the search visibility problem.
