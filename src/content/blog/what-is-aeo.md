# What Is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

Answer Engine Optimization, or AEO, is the practice of optimizing content so that it can effectively answer questions in search and AI-powered experiences.

Traditional SEO often focuses on rankings.

AEO focuses more heavily on answers.

## Why did AEO become important?

Search engines have increasingly moved toward answering questions directly.

People can now see answers through:

- Featured snippets
- AI Overviews
- Voice assistants
- AI search platforms
- Conversational search

This changes what businesses need to optimize for.

## How does AEO work?

AEO starts with understanding the questions your audience is asking.

Then you create content that provides a direct, useful, and clearly structured answer.

Useful techniques include:

- Question-based content
- Clear headings
- Concise answers
- Structured information
- Relevant supporting evidence
- Strong topical context

## AEO, SEO, and GEO

These concepts overlap.

SEO focuses heavily on organic search visibility.

AEO focuses on providing useful answers.

GEO focuses on visibility within generative search experiences.

For modern businesses, these strategies can work together rather than being treated as completely separate disciplines.

The underlying principle is simple:

**Create information that search systems can understand and users can trust.**
