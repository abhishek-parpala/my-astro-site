# SEO vs GEO: What's the Difference?

Search is changing.

Traditional SEO focuses on helping websites rank in search engine results. Generative Engine Optimization, or GEO, focuses on helping brands become visible when AI systems generate answers.

## What is SEO?

SEO helps search engines understand, discover, and rank your website.

It usually involves:

- Keyword research
- Content creation
- Technical SEO
- Internal linking
- Backlink building
- Improving search intent alignment

The goal is usually to earn organic clicks from search results.

## What is GEO?

GEO focuses on improving how a brand appears in AI-generated answers.

This includes visibility across systems such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI features.

GEO can involve:

- Building brand authority
- Creating useful, citable content
- Earning relevant mentions
- Improving entity clarity
- Building a strong presence across the wider web

## SEO and GEO are not competitors

For most businesses, the two approaches work together.

SEO helps your website become discoverable and authoritative.

GEO expands that visibility into the growing world of AI-powered search.

The future of search is likely to involve both.
